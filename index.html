Research

My current research focuses on optimizers for large-scale language model pre-training. I study the mathematical principles underlying training dynamics across model scales, with related work on hyperparameter transfer and scaling-aware parameterizations such as μP. I am also interested in efficient architectures for sequence modeling, and the interplay between architecture and optimization. My earlier work spans transfer learning, 3D learning, and streaming algorithms.
